# Decision log

Record decisions that will affect future work. Do not use this file as a general activity log.

<!-- Example scale: choosing in-memory storage for v1 is durable; renaming a local variable is not. -->

## ADR-001: Decision title

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Status: proposed / accepted / superseded
- Context: Why is a decision needed?
- Decision: What was chosen?
- Rationale: Why was it chosen?
- Consequences: Benefits, costs, migration, and compatibility effects.
- Alternatives: What else was considered?
- Supersedes: None, or a link to the earlier decision.
